Frequently asked questions

Where do the events come from?

From the public event listings on OneIllinois, where UIUC clubs post their events. More sources are planned.

How does the site decide an event has free food?

An event is listed if the club marked "food provided" on OneIllinois, or if its listing mentions food such as pizza, snacks, refreshments, or lunch. Food drives and food pantry events are left out, since they collect food rather than give it away.

How often is the list updated?

Four times a day, at about 7 and 11 a.m. and 3 and 7 p.m. (an hour earlier in winter). Events disappear from the list once they end.

Can I see events on other days?

Yes. The Calendar page shows the next two weeks. Tap a day to see its events. The Today page also lists the next few days under "Coming up", and events that already ended today under "Earlier today".

How do the filters work?

Open Filters above the list. Meals covers listings that mention things like pizza, tacos, lunch, or dinner; Snacks covers things like cookies, boba, or refreshments. When a club only says food is provided, the event shows under both. A time range shows every event that overlaps it by at least a minute, so a 4:30 to 5:15 event appears for 5 to 6. Filters reset when you leave the page.

Is the food open to everyone?

That's up to each club. Some events are for members or need an RSVP. Tap the event name to open its OneIllinois page and check.

Why does an event say "Location shown after signing in"?

OneIllinois hides the location of some events from people who aren't signed in. Open the event and sign in with your NetID to see it.

Why isn't an event I know about listed?

It may not be on OneIllinois, or its listing may not mention food. If you know of one that should be here, let us know.

Who runs this?

A UIUC student. The site is not affiliated with the University of Illinois.
