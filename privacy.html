Privacy

You can use everything on this site without an account. This page explains what happens if you make one.

What we store

When you sign in with your @illinois.edu Google account, we store your name and email address. Google tells us these when you sign in. We don't see or store your password.

When you save an event, we store a copy of that event's listing with your account so it shows on any device. Saved events are deleted a week after the event ends.

When you follow a club, we store the club's name with your account until you unfollow it or delete your account.

If you turn on Google Calendar on the Account page, the site creates a calendar called "Free Food @ UIUC" in your Google account and adds the events you save to it. Google only lets the site see and change that one calendar, not the rest of your Google Calendar. We store that calendar's ID with your account. Google's access is kept only in your browser tab for about an hour, never on our servers. Turning it off deletes the calendar and gives up the site's access.

When you submit an event, we store its details with your account so you can see whether it was approved. The site's admins review it, and your name isn't shown with the event. Submissions are deleted a week after the event ends.

When you upload a flyer, it's sent to Google's Gemini AI to read its details, then discarded; we don't keep it. Google may use uploads to improve its products, and its reviewers may read them, so don't upload anything with personal information. We also count how many flyers you upload each day to enforce a daily limit, and clear those counts within two days.

Why

To know which account is yours, so features like saving events can work. We don't sell your information or show you ads, and we only share what's described on this page.

Where it's kept

Accounts are stored with Supabase, a database service. Sign-in and Google Calendar go through Google, and flyers you upload go to Google's Gemini AI.

Deleting your account

On the Account page, choose "Delete my account". Your name, email, saved events, and followed clubs are removed right away. Events you submitted stay in the review queue and on the site, no longer connected to you. If you turned on Google Calendar, turn it off first to delete the "Free Food @ UIUC" calendar. Deleting your account can't reach your Google account, so the calendar would stay there.
